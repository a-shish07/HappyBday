Written for Priya 25 November · 23.41

Hi, Priya.

Priya, you have given me so many valuable lessons. From what was wrong and what was right, from little things I may not have noticed before, to the things that ultimately helped me become a better person.

Happy Birthday, Priya.

I wrote this letter on November 15 at 11:41. Honestly, I do not know whether these flowers will arrive on your birthday. If they arrive late, I am sorry. Still, I hope this letter reaches you and is read with a smile.

On November 25, you grow one year older. In this new year of your life, may every good thing increase—happiness, health, prosperity, and may the hopes you have kept slowly become real.

May your smile always remain, and may life always treat you gently.

Thank you for being part of my life and helping me become better. Maybe time has taken us down different paths, but the memories and lessons we shared will always have their own place in my life.

I always hope you are surrounded by good people, meet good things, and are kept away from anything that could make you sad.

I am sorry that I can only give a simple gift like this. It may not be much, but I prepared it sincerely. I hope you like it.

I do not know how far life will carry each of us. But one thing is certain: I will always wish the very best for you.

Happy birthday, Priya.

Thank you for every story, lesson, and memory we shared.

Priya, if you ever think that no one has loved you, remember this: my feelings have not changed at all from 2023 until now.

Maybe many things have changed. Time, circumstances, and our busy lives have changed. But in that regard, I am still the same person.

So if one day something is wrong, you are sad, tired, or simply need someone to talk to, do not forget that you can find me. My WhatsApp number will never change.

Oh, and once you have read this, you do not need to message me to say thank you. If the flowers reach you, a little hint in a WhatsApp status is enough.

If you do not like the flowers, it is okay to let them go. I understand the situation now. They are simply a sign that I still have the same feelings, only in a better form.

Once again, happy birthday, Priya. May today be the beginning of many happy things waiting ahead.

with all my heart, Ashish Dubey
